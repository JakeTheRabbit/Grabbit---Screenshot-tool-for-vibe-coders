# New picture editor (beta)

What someone who has just taken a screenshot wants to do, and what the new editor does about it. Built on
[Fabric.js](https://fabricjs.com) (MIT). The capture *is* the canvas: no endless whiteboard, no panels over the picture.

## Point at things
- **Arrow** (A), **line** (L): drag; drag either end to re-aim; Shift snaps to 45°.
- **Box** (R), **ellipse** (O): drag; Shift for a square / circle; fill none, see-through or solid.
- **Numbered steps** (N): click to drop 1, 2, 3…; numbering carries on from the highest.
- **Highlighter** (H): a marker you drag over text; the text stays readable underneath.
- **Pen** (P): freehand.
- **Text** (T): click and type; optional background so it reads on any picture.
- **Stickers**: emoji and icons, at a size you can see.
- **Magnify** (M): drag over a detail; a 2× zoomed copy appears beside it.

## Hide things
- **Pixelate** (X), **blur** (B), **black out** (K): drag over it. Switch an area between them later.
- **Hide private details**: finds emails, keys, phone and card numbers in the text and pixelates them.
- **Spotlight** (S): drag round what matters; everything else dims.

## Fix the picture
- **Crop** (C): drag the part to keep, Enter to apply.
- **Eyedropper** (I): pick a colour from the picture for your next shape.
- **Insert** another capture on top, or paste any picture (Ctrl+V).
- AI: remove the background, remove or change what's in a box, rewrite text (your key).

## Work comfortably
- Thin lines are easy to grab (generous hit area), any shape moves, resizes and rotates.
- Colour, width, fill, text size and shadow apply to the selection, or to what you draw next.
- Undo / redo everything (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), delete, duplicate (Ctrl+D), nudge with the arrow keys, bring to front / send back.
- Zoom: fits the window; Ctrl+wheel to zoom, Space or middle-drag to pan, 0 to fit, 1 for 100 %.
- Every tool has a key; Esc goes back to Select.
- Tabs for several captures; the capture list to insert or open more; toolbar on any side.

## Get it out
- Copy (full, smaller, chat-sized), copy and close, save, save as, copy path, pin, share link, Talk to AI, copy text,
  read QR, Beautify (background, corners, shadow, social sizes). Exports at the capture's own resolution.
