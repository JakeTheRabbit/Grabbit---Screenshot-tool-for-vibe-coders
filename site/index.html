Download Grabbit for desktop.

A free, open-source screenshot and screen recorder for Windows, macOS and Linux. Tap PrtScn for a screenshot, hold it to record. Talk over a capture and paste the picture, your words and the text on screen into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a coding agent.

What Grabbit does.

Each clip is a short narrated walkthrough. They play without sound; press Sound to hear the narration.

PrtScn

An action wheel at the pointer.

Drag over an area and a wheel of actions opens where your mouse is. Enter copies. Every slice has a number key and most have a letter: E marks up, R records, V talks to AI, N keeps the area and lets you add another.

Press PrtScn again while the wheel is up and a second capture opens on top of the first, which is handy for how-to guides.

V

Talk to AI about what's on screen.

Press V, click to point at things, drag to box them, and say what you mean. Your words appear in a box you can fix. Ctrl+Enter copies the picture with your words under it, the numbered marks, and the text on screen, so the AI can quote exact labels and numbers.

It works on recordings too: key frames from the moments you spoke, with a timestamped transcript.

M

More in the More menu.

  • Scrolling capture of long pages, stitched into one picture
  • Copy the text in an area as plain text, Markdown, CSV or a table
  • Read a QR code, pin a picture on top of every window
  • Describe and tag it with AI, start a batch of screenshots with notes
  • Capture again in 3 or 10 seconds
E

Mark it up.

Arrows, boxes, text, numbered steps that count themselves, spotlight, blur, crop and emoji. Every tool has a one-letter key. Beautify adds padding, a background and a shadow for sharing.

Editor

Hide private details in one click.

Grabbit finds and pixelates emails, IP addresses, phone and card numbers, API keys and tokens before you share.

Hold PrtScn

Record the screen.

Hold PrtScn for two seconds and pick an area or a window. A countdown, then it records. The red frame and the Stop bar never show up in the video. Pause, move the frame, or start over.

System sound and microphone go on separate tracks, and every recording gets a notes folder with a transcript, for you or a coding agent that can't watch video.

After recording

Edit the video.

A monitor with timecode, video and sound tracks with a waveform, and an icon for each tool that shows its name and key on hover.

  • Cut silences in one click, add captions made on your computer
  • Split, trim, speed a part up
  • Zoom in on your clicks, show click rings and the keys you pressed
  • Copy small enough for chat, or as a GIF
Tray menu

Find any capture again.

History searches the words in every picture, what was said in every recording, the apps that were on screen, and where you pasted it. pasted:slack finds everything you sent to Slack.

Settings

Run a command after each capture.

Run a command or post to a webhook after every capture: file it into Paperless, a synced folder, n8n, Zapier or Home Assistant.

    Other features.

    Ready to paste anywhere.

    Every capture is on the clipboard as a picture and a named file, and saved as lossless WebP at about 40% of PNG's size.

    Up to 8 areas at once.

    Across all your screens, kept separate or joined side by side, stacked or in a grid.

    Replay buffer.

    Keeps the last 10 seconds to 10 minutes of your screen; one key saves it.

    Step recorder.

    Click through a task and get a how-to as Markdown and a Word document.

    Share.

    Upload to OneDrive, Immich, MEGA or any S3 storage and copy the link, or send to Obsidian.

    Invisible watermark.

    Your name and the capture's id hidden in the pixels, surviving JPEG and cropping.

    Private by default.

    Nothing leaves your computer until you press Share or an AI button. AI uses your own key or a free local model.

    Updates itself.

    New versions download quietly and install when you say so. Updates are signed.

    For developers.

    Coding agents can use your captures.

    Run grabbit --mcp or add it from Settings, and Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or T3 Code can search every screenshot and recording by its text, open one, read a recording's transcript, or take a screenshot themselves.

    Small.

    A 20 MB installer. Idle, it's one process using about 15 MB of memory. Rust core with the system webview, no Electron. No account, no ads.

    Fast.

    The screens freeze the moment you press the key, straight from the graphics card. The wheel opens in under a second, or in about 0.1 s with Instant capture on, which keeps a few hundred MB loaded.

    Keyboard first.

    Every action has a key, and every key can be changed. grabbit://capture, grabbit://record and the same words on the command line for launchers and scripts.

    Recordings an agent can read.

    Each recording gets a notes folder with transcript.md, captions, stills and recording.json.

    Text out of pictures.

    Copy the text in any area as plain text, Markdown, CSV, HTML or a table. Read QR codes.

    Scriptable.

    Run a command or post a webhook after every capture, with the file path, the apps on screen and the text in it.

    Open source.

    MIT licence, built and tested in public on GitHub Actions for Windows, macOS and Linux.

    Compared with other apps.

    From each app's own website and changelog, checked in November 2026. Prices in US dollars. If something here is wrong, open an issue and it will be fixed.

    Install and questions.

    How do I install it on Windows?

    Installer (.exe) or .msi, for Windows 10 and 11. Grabbit isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so SmartScreen asks once: choose More info, then Run anyway.

    With Scoop: scoop bucket add grabbit https://github.com/JakeTheRabbit/Grabbit---Screenshot-tool-for-vibe-coders, then scoop install grabbit/grabbit.

    Recording needs ffmpeg: winget install Gyan.FFmpeg.

    How do I install it on a Mac?

    Apple silicon (.dmg) or Intel (.dmg), for macOS 13 and later. Drag it to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time. Allow Screen Recording and Microphone in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

    With Homebrew: brew tap jaketherabbit/grabbit https://github.com/JakeTheRabbit/Grabbit---Screenshot-tool-for-vibe-coders, then brew install --cask jaketherabbit/grabbit/grabbit.

    Recording needs ffmpeg: brew install ffmpeg.

    How do I install it on Linux?

    AppImage, .deb or .rpm. Needs a 2024 or newer distro (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40). For the AppImage: chmod +x Grabbit*.AppImage, then run it.

    Recording needs ffmpeg: sudo apt install ffmpeg tesseract-ocr on Debian and Ubuntu. The .deb installs both for you.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Grabbit is free and open source under the MIT licence. There's no account, no paid plan and no watermark.

    Does anything get sent online?

    Nothing leaves your computer until you press Share or an AI button. AI features use your own key or a local model. Metadata is stripped from captures by default.

    How do I connect it to Claude Code, Codex or Cursor?

    Open Settings, Coding agents, and press Add to Claude Code or Add to Codex. For other tools, press Copy setup and paste it into the tool's MCP settings. The server is grabbit --mcp.

    Why does it use so little memory?

    When idle Grabbit is a single small process. The capture windows load when you press the key. Turn on Instant capture in Settings if you'd rather trade a few hundred MB of memory for a wheel that opens in about 0.1 s.
