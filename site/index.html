Grabbit

A free, open-source screenshot and screen-recording app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Press PrtScn for a screenshot, hold it for a recording. Screenshots can carry your spoken notes and the text on screen, ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a coding agent.

⬇ Download Grabbit Free and open source (MIT). No account, no watermark. Other systems

Full tour

6 minutes 21 seconds, narrated, with a chapter for each feature.

    Features

    Each clip is a short narrated walkthrough. Clips play without sound; press Sound to hear the narration.

    Action wheel

    After you drag over an area, a wheel of actions opens centred on the pointer. Enter copies. Every slice has a number key and most have a letter: E marks up, R records, V talks to AI, N keeps the area and lets you add another.

    Press PrtScn again while the wheel is up and a second capture opens on top of the first, which is handy for how-to guides.

    Talk to AI

    Press V, click to point at things, drag to box them, and say what you mean. Your words appear in a box you can fix. Ctrl+Enter copies the picture with your words printed under it, the numbered marks, and the text on screen, so the AI can quote exact labels and numbers.

    It works on recordings too: key frames from the moments you spoke, with a timestamped transcript.

    More menu

    • Scrolling capture of long pages, stitched into one picture
    • Copy the text in an area (as plain text, Markdown, CSV or a table)
    • Read a QR code, pin a picture on top of every window
    • Describe and tag it with AI, start a batch of screenshots with notes
    • Capture again in 3 or 10 seconds

    Markup editor

    Arrows, boxes, text, numbered steps that count themselves, spotlight, blur, crop, emoji. Every tool has a one-letter key. Beautify adds padding, a background and a shadow for sharing.

    Hide private details

    One click finds and pixelates emails, IP addresses, phone and card numbers, API keys and tokens.

    Screen recording

    Hold PrtScn for two seconds and pick an area or a window. A countdown, then it records. The red frame and the Stop bar never show up in the video. Pause, move the frame, or start over.

    System sound and microphone are kept on separate tracks, and every recording gets a notes folder with a transcript, for you or a coding agent that can't watch video.

    Video editor

    A monitor with timecode, video and sound tracks with a waveform, and an icon for each tool that shows its name and key on hover.

    • Cut silences in one click, add captions made on your computer
    • Split, trim, speed a part up
    • Zoom in on your clicks, show click rings and the keys you pressed
    • Copy small enough for chat, or as a GIF

    History

    History searches the words in every picture, what was said in every recording, the apps that were on screen, and where you pasted it. pasted:slack finds everything you sent to Slack.

    Automations

    After every capture, run a command or post to a webhook: file it into Paperless, a synced folder, n8n, Zapier or Home Assistant.

    Other features

    Ready to paste anywhere
    Every capture is on the clipboard as a picture and a named file, and saved as lossless WebP at about 40% of PNG's size.
    Up to 8 areas at once
    Across all your screens, kept separate or joined side by side, stacked or in a grid.
    Replay buffer
    Always keeps the last 10 seconds to 10 minutes; one key saves it.
    Step recorder
    Click through a task and get a how-to as Markdown and a Word document.
    Share
    Upload to OneDrive, Immich, MEGA or any S3 storage and copy the link, or send to Obsidian.
    Invisible watermark
    Your name and the capture's id hidden in the pixels, surviving JPEG and cropping.
    Private by default
    Nothing leaves your computer until you press Share or an AI button. AI uses your own key or a free local model.
    Updates itself
    New versions download quietly and install when you say so.

    For developers

    Fast
    The screens freeze about a tenth of a second after the key press on Windows, even with four screens. Frames come straight from the graphics card through shared memory.
    Small
    A 20 MB installer. Rust core, no Electron, no bundled browser. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no upsell screens.
    Keyboard first
    Every action has a key, and every key can be changed. grabbit://capture, grabbit://record and the same words on the command line for launchers and scripts.
    Made for coding agents
    Talk to AI copies the picture, your words, numbered marks and the screen's text in one paste. Each recording gets a notes folder with transcript.md, captions, stills and recording.json, so an agent that can't watch video can still read it.
    Text out of pictures
    Copy the text in any area as plain text, Markdown, CSV, HTML or a table. Read QR codes. Search every capture by its text.
    Scriptable
    Run a command or post a webhook after every capture, with the file path, the apps on screen and the text in it.
    Private
    Everything stays on your computer until you press Share or an AI button. AI uses your own key or a local model. Metadata is stripped by default.
    Open source
    MIT licence, built and tested in public on GitHub Actions for Windows, macOS and Linux. Updates are signed.

    Install

    Grabbit isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows and macOS ask once before the first run. The source and the builds are public on GitHub.

    Windows 10 and 11Installer (.exe) · .msi
    SmartScreen: More info, then Run anyway.
    macOS 13 and laterApple silicon (.dmg) · Intel (.dmg)
    Drag it to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. Allow Screen Recording and Microphone in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    LinuxAppImage · .deb · .rpm
    Needs a 2024 or newer distro (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40). For the AppImage: chmod +x Grabbit*.AppImage, then run it.

    Recording needs ffmpeg: winget install Gyan.FFmpeg on Windows, brew install ffmpeg on macOS, sudo apt install ffmpeg tesseract-ocr on Debian and Ubuntu (the .deb installs both for you).
